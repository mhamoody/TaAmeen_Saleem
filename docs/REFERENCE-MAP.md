# Saleem landing page — reference map

Reviewed 5–6 November 2026. Sources: the supplied **Saleem Developer Brief – Motor** (dated 5 November), public Tameeni pages and public Bcare entry screens. **Shory was skipped at the user's request.** No personal data, quotation requests, payment or support messages were submitted to either reference website.

## What this delivery covers

One Arabic, responsive landing page with all ten categories requested by the employer. Product selection and journey previews are local UI interactions. The short journey descriptions are proposed navigation summaries, not implementations of the full purchase flows.

The brief describes a much larger platform and distinguishes confirmed requirements, observations and recommendations. Its motor journey informs the design; its identity verification, insurer APIs, payments, policy issuance, accounts and back office remain outside this landing-page task. Showing ten categories does **not** mean ten products are approved or available for launch: the brief leaves launch products for management approval and states a maximum of three during the pilot.

## Observed entry patterns

- Tameeni groups products into individual/business audiences and links each category to its own introduction and application. Bcare switches its product navigation between individuals and businesses. The Saleem page uses audience filtering while keeping an “all” view.
- Motor is a coverage family, not three separate products. The brief groups issue/renewal, transfer and customs-card cases as purposes. Bcare separates purpose from vehicle registration type. The Saleem preview follows the brief's three-purpose structure.
- Tameeni presents third-party, plus and comprehensive cover on its public motor page. Bcare labels its middle category “مميز”. These labels are not evidence of identical benefits: Saleem's preview does not imply equivalent cover or invent prices.
- Bcare's initial forms show identity fields, consent and a verification challenge. Motor additionally asks for vehicle identification; business medical starts with a commercial/unified number; malpractice includes a start date; domestic medical includes sponsor identity and birth date. No such personal data is collected in this landing-page prototype.
- Tameeni's cargo page describes single-shipment cover for three transport modes and import/export/domestic movement. Its fleet page advertises a small-fleet journey; that competitor-specific limit is not imposed as a Saleem rule.
- Account, policy printing/cancellation, claims assistance, vehicle-history reports, financed-car services and retail offers are supporting services or channels, not additional categories in the ten-product landing-page inventory.

The design uses original copy and layout. It does not copy competitors' customer counts, discounts, insurer relationships, ratings, licensing statements, instant-issuance promises or service guarantees.

## Future implementation boundary

Before adding real transactions, confirm the approved products and insurer specifications, identity/consent flow, coverage wording and operational contacts with Saleem. The brief's open decisions and integration requirements remain decisions for the full project, rather than implied commitments in this frontend page.
